3 # Part I — Design Objectives
4
5 ### O1 — Excitation Integrity
6
7 The delivered drive current equals the commanded current at every (current, frequency) in the ICG and BCA schedules on
a body-like load, and the board itself consumes only a small, known share of the compliance window.
8
9 ### O2 — Receive-Path Fidelity on Body-Like Loads
10
11 The sense path rejects the common-mode carrier that a person (not a resistor) produces, so impedance on a person is as
accurate as on a bench load.
12
13 ### O3 — Noise Floor: Broadband and Spurs
14
15 The board adds little to the chip's own noise in the ICG band and adds no discrete lines.
16
17 ### O4 — Calibration Accuracy and Traceability
18
19 Calibration is traceable, covers the clinical impedance band, and states which parts of the electrode path are inside
the correction.
20
21 ### O5 — Long-Duration Stability
22
23 Impedance on a fixed load is stable over a full clinical session after a short, declared warm-up, and drift can be
attributed.
24
25 ### O6 — Low-Noise Power Tree
26
27 Supply noise from the motherboard and isolated converter does not reach the AFE, digital activity does not modulate
AVDD/VREF, and the isolated rail has margin.
28
29 ### O7 — Layout and Grounding Integrity
30
31 The copper gives every analog signal a continuous return, keeps drive away from sense, keeps the sense pair symmetric,
and keeps aggressors away from the AFE.
32
33 ### O8 — Observability and Testability
34
35 The things we argue about can be measured on a built module without touching the CM4 or motherboard.
36
37 ### O9 — Patient Safety and Protection
38
39 Every electrode meets the single-fault DC limit, protection is designed and pulse-tested, and every new element that
crosses the isolation barrier is rated for it.
40
41 ### O10 — Compatibility and Drop-In Constraint
42
43 The V4 module is a drop-in replacement: same header and pin functions, no CM4 or ADS1293 change, and old firmware
cannot produce a silently wrong result on it.
